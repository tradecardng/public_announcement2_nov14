We believe in being open with you, so we want to share an update. Between 4 and 9 November, TradeCard's systems were targeted in a cyberattack. During that time, some orders were delayed, and some were lost. It's now fully resolved, and here's what you should know.

The issue is fixed, and TradeCard is running normally again.
Your TradeCard account and balance are safe.
There's nothing you need to do.
WHAT HAPPENED

A global attack that reached TradeCard

Recently, a serious security weakness was found in PHP, a technology used by a huge number of websites and apps around the world. This weakness, widely known as PHP2Shell, led to a wave of cyberattacks on many companies worldwide.

Between 4 and 9 November, TradeCard's systems were targeted in a similar attack. Here's what happened, step by step:

  1. 1
    Our security system detected the attackIt flagged unusual activity on our order system.
  2. 2
    Order processing was stopped to protect the systemTo keep the platform and account safe, the system paused order processing. As a result, many orders were delayed, and some were lost.
  3. Our team worked round the clockWe fixed the issue, secured the system and restored order processing, and we recovered as many orders as we could.
  4. TradeCard is running normally againOrders are being processed as usual, and account and balance are safe.
